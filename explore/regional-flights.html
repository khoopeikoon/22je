Bali, Phuket and Bangkok

Changi Airport is 25 minutes from the house by car, with flights across the region.

Much of Southeast Asia is a short flight away: Phuket is about two hours from Singapore, and Bangkok and Bali under three.

Book early for public holiday weekends, when fares jump. Check visa rules for your nationality, and make sure your Singapore pass is valid for your return.
