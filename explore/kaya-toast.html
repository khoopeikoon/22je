Kaya toast and kopi

A traditional Singapore breakfast: toast, coconut jam, soft-boiled eggs and coffee.

Kaya is a sweet coconut jam, served on toast with butter. Crack two soft-boiled eggs into a saucer, add dark soy sauce and white pepper, and dip.

Order your coffee like a local: kopi comes with condensed milk, kopi-O is black with sugar, and kopi-C has evaporated milk. Toast Box and Ya Kun are both at Paragon nearby, and we recommend the Ya Kun in Paragon’s basement. Killiney Kopitiam on Killiney Road is the classic choice, but it’s a walk of about 19 minutes (1.4 km), and we don’t think it’s worth the walk.

Good to know: at busy food courts and hawker centres, people often reserve (“chope”) a table by leaving something on it, such as a tissue packet, a card or a water bottle. Keep anything valuable with you. Read about chope culture →
