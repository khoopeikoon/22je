Lau Pa Sat

A Victorian cast-iron market hall among the office towers of the CBD.

The octagonal hall dates from 1894 and is now a food centre with a wide mix of stalls, busy at weekday lunchtimes.

In the evening, part of Boon Tat Street closes to traffic and fills with satay grills and outdoor tables.

Good to know: at busy food courts and hawker centres, people often reserve (“chope”) a table by leaving something on it, such as a tissue packet, a card or a water bottle. Keep anything valuable with you. Read about chope culture →

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. North–South Line to Raffles Place (4 stops), then a walk of about 5 minutes.
