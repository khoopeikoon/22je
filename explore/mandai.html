Mandai Wildlife Reserve

Five wildlife parks in one rainforest reserve, including Singapore Zoo.

Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, River Wonders, Bird Paradise and Rainforest Wild Adventure sit side by side at Mandai. The zoo is known for its open habitats, and the Night Safari lets you watch nocturnal animals from a tram after dark.

Each park has its own ticket, with bundles if you want more than one. One park can take most of a day, so allow plenty of time.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. Thomson–East Coast Line to Springleaf, no change, then bus 138. Or North–South Line to Khatib, then the M2 Khatib bus. Check the last bus if you’re staying for the Night Safari.
