Chinatown

Temples, shophouses and hawker food, right beside the skyscrapers of the CBD.

Visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, then Sri Mariamman Temple, Singapore’s oldest Hindu temple, a short walk away. The market streets around Pagoda Street sell souvenirs, and Chinatown Complex has one of the city’s biggest hawker centres.

Before Chinese New Year, the area fills with lanterns and festive stalls. On Telok Ayer Street, nearby, you’ll find the historic Thian Hock Keng temple.
