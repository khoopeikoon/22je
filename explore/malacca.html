Malacca

A UNESCO World Heritage city in Malaysia, full of history and Peranakan food.

Malacca’s old town has Dutch and Portuguese buildings, Jonker Street’s night market, Peranakan cooking and chicken rice balls. The historic core is compact enough to explore on foot.

Coaches from Singapore take about four to five hours, including the border crossing, and can take longer at busy times. Bring your passport, and avoid the public holiday rush if you can.

  • Getting thereA coach from Singapore. Several operators sell tickets online and leave from different points around the city.
