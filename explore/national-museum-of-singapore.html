National Museum of Singapore

Singapore’s oldest museum, telling the story of how the country came to be.

The Singapore History Gallery takes you from the region’s early trading days through colonial rule, the Japanese Occupation and independence. The building itself, with its stained-glass dome, dates from 1887.

Allow a few hours to see the history gallery properly.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. North–South Line to Dhoby Ghaut (2 stops), then a walk of about 6 minutes.
