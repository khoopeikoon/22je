Merlion Park

Singapore’s half-lion, half-fish mascot, with the whole Marina Bay skyline behind it.

The 8.6-metre Merlion spouts water into the bay from a small waterfront park in front of The Fullerton Hotel. Allow about 30 minutes, more if it’s crowded.

Come early in the morning for space to take a photo, or at dusk when the skyline lights up. Then carry on around the bay on foot: the waterfront promenade links up with the Esplanade and Marina Bay Sands.

  • Getting thereNorth–South Line to Raffles Place (4 stops), then a walk of about 11 minutes.
