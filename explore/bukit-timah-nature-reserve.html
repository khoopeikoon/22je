Bukit Timah Nature Reserve

Primary rainforest and Singapore’s highest hill, at the edge of the city.

At 163 metres, Bukit Timah Hill is Singapore’s highest natural peak, in one of its few primary rainforests. Several trails lead to the summit.

Go in the morning and bring water. Keep food out of sight because of the macaques, and watch for colugos gliding between the trees. NParks has a notice about demolition works at the summit, so check it before you go.
