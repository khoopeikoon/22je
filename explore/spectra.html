Spectra at Marina Bay Sands

A free nightly show of fountains, lasers and projections on the waterfront.

Spectra plays out over the water at the Event Plaza in front of The Shoppes, with music, jets of water and light. It lasts about 15 minutes, and you don’t need a ticket.

It runs at 8 pm and 9 pm, with a 10 pm show on Fridays and Saturdays. Arrive at least 15 minutes early, and check the official page in case times change.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. Thomson–East Coast Line to Marina Bay, then the Circle Line to Bayfront.
