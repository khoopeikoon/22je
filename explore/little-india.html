Little India

Spice shops, flower garlands and colourful shophouses along Serangoon Road.

Start at Tekka Centre, for its market and food stalls, then walk up Serangoon Road to Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple. Side streets are full of goldsmiths, sari shops and restaurants serving dishes from across India.

Weekends can be crowded, and Deepavali brings street lights and bazaars. Dress modestly if you plan to go inside the temples.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. North–South Line to Dhoby Ghaut (2 stops), then the North East Line to Little India, one stop.
