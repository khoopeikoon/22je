Haw Par Villa

A park of Chinese folklore statues, built by the Tiger Balm brothers.

Opened in 1937, the park has more than a thousand painted statues and dioramas of myths and moral tales. The Ten Courts of Hell, in Hell’s Museum, are as graphic as they sound, so think twice before bringing small children.

Parts of the park have been closed for maintenance since December 2025, while Hell’s Museum stays open, so check the official site before you go.
