Maxwell Food Centre

A hawker centre in Chinatown, and home of Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice.

Tian Tian is its best-known stall, and others sell dishes from fish porridge to Fuzhou oyster cake.

Come before noon or after 2 pm to avoid the lunch crowd. Chinatown is right beside it.

Good to know: at busy food courts and hawker centres, people often reserve (“chope”) a table by leaving something on it, such as a tissue packet, a card or a water bottle. Keep anything valuable with you. Read about chope culture →

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. Thomson–East Coast Line to Maxwell, no change. The food centre is by the station.
