Bugis Street

A busy covered market for cheap clothes, snacks and souvenirs.

Billed as Singapore’s largest street-shopping location, Bugis Street is a warren of small stalls selling inexpensive fashion, accessories and gifts.

Bugis Junction and Bugis+ are across the road when you want air conditioning, and Kampong Glam is within walking distance.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. North–South Line to City Hall (3 stops), then the East–West Line to Bugis, one stop.
