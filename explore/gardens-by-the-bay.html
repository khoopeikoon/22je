Gardens by the Bay

Giant Supertrees and a misty indoor mountain, beside Marina Bay.

The Supertree Grove is free to wander. The two big glass conservatories are ticketed: Cloud Forest wraps one of the world’s tallest indoor waterfalls in a plant-covered mountain, while Flower Dome holds the Guinness World Record for the largest glass greenhouse, and is pleasantly cool on a hot afternoon.

Give yourself plenty of time: arrive by late afternoon, check the conservatories’ last entry, and stay on for the free Garden Rhapsody light show after dark.
