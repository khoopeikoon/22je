East Coast Park

Singapore’s long seaside park, for cycling and picnics.

The park stretches for about 13 km along the shore, with a track for cycling and skating. You can rent bikes inside the park and watch the ships anchored offshore.

Finish at East Coast Lagoon Food Village, a hawker centre by the beach.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. Thomson–East Coast Line to Marine Parade, no change, then a walk of about 7 minutes.
