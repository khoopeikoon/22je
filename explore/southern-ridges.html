The Southern Ridges

A walk of about 10 km along the green hilltops of the south, much of it above the trees.

The route links Mount Faber, Telok Blangah Hill and Kent Ridge through forest walkways. The highlight is Henderson Waves, Singapore’s highest pedestrian bridge at 36 metres, with its wooden curves.

If you’re not sure about the full route, start with the stretch from Mount Faber to Henderson Waves, though part of it has been diverted for maintenance, so check NParks first. Bring water, and avoid the hottest part of the day.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. Thomson–East Coast Line to Outram Park, North East Line to HarbourFront, then walk up the Marang Trail to Mount Faber.
