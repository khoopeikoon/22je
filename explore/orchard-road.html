Orchard Road

Singapore’s famous shopping boulevard, a 5-minute walk from your front door.

Along about two and a half kilometres of malls, you’ll find everything from luxury flagships at ION Orchard, Paragon and Ngee Ann City to everyday shops and food courts in the basements.

Come between mid-November and early January for the Christmas lights. When it’s raining, underground links connect many of the malls.
