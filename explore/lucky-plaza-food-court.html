Lucky Plaza food court

A 6th-floor food court on Orchard Road that brings together many of Singapore’s best-known dishes. We think it’s worth a visit.

The food court (#06-48A) had 12 stalls when it opened in 2024, including Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice, Tenderbest, Nasi Lemak Berlauk Melaka and Lion City Dim Sum, though stalls can change. On the same floor, My Favourite Cafe (#06-046) sells yong tau foo and only accepts cash.

It’s busiest at weekday lunchtimes. For the shortest queues and the easiest time, go at the weekend, before 11:30 am or after 1:30 pm. Stall hours vary, so check before you go, and bring cash. On Sundays the food court stays quiet, but the area around the building gets crowded, as many domestic workers spend their day off at Lucky Plaza.

Good to know: at busy food courts and hawker centres, people often reserve (“chope”) a table by leaving something on it, such as a tissue packet, a card or a water bottle. Keep anything valuable with you. Read about chope culture →
