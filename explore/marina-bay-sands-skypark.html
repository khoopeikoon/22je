Marina Bay Sands SkyPark

The view from the ship-shaped deck on top of Marina Bay Sands.

The SkyPark Observation Deck looks out over the city, Gardens by the Bay and the durian-shaped domes of the Esplanade. Entry is ticketed and open to everyone; the famous infinity pool is for hotel guests only.

Evenings from 5 pm are the peak period and cost more, so book a timed ticket ahead. Afterwards you’re right by The Shoppes and the ArtScience Museum.

  • Getting thereFrom the house, walk 10 minutes to Orchard MRT. Thomson–East Coast Line to Marina Bay, then the Circle Line to Bayfront.
