MacRitchie TreeTop Walk

A 250-metre suspension bridge through the rainforest canopy, in the middle of a nature reserve.

The bridge is 25 metres up at its highest, between two hills in the reserve. You have to hike there: NParks puts the 7 km loop from Windsor Nature Park at 3 to 4 hours, so wear proper shoes and bring water.

The bridge is closed on Mondays, except public holidays, and last entry is at 4:45 pm, so set off early. NParks says the trail isn’t suitable for young children or people with a fear of heights. Keep food out of sight because of the long-tailed macaques.

  • Getting thereEasiest by taxi or ride-hailing to Windsor Nature Park, off Venus Drive, as no MRT station is close.
