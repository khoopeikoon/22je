Emerald Hill

Restored Peranakan terrace houses and bars, just off Orchard Road.

This short street of early-1900s terrace houses, many in Chinese Baroque style, was once home to Singapore’s wealthy Peranakan community. Peranakan Place, with its bars, is at the foot of the hill.

The houses higher up are private homes, so please enjoy them from the street.

  • Getting thereA walk of about 13 minutes from the house. Somerset MRT is close by, one stop from Orchard.
