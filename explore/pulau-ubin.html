Pulau Ubin

Cycle around a small island of old quarries and forest tracks.

A bumboat ride of about 10 minutes from Changi takes you to the island. You can rent a bicycle in the village and ride out to Chek Jawa, a wetland on its south-eastern tip.

Bring cash for the boat and bikes, as there are no ATMs on the island, plus water and sun protection. There’s no timetable: boats leave once they have enough passengers, usually 9 to 12, and run until about 7 pm, so allow plenty of time to get back.

  • Getting thereTaxi or bus to Changi Point Ferry Terminal, then a bumboat of about 10 minutes.
