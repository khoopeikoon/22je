Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve

Mangroves and mudflats in the far north, with herons, otters and wild crocodiles.

Boardwalks run through the mangroves. From August to April, migratory shorebirds such as whimbrels and greenshanks stop here, while monitor lizards can be seen all year.

Estuarine crocodiles live here, so stay on the paths. Some areas close at high tide, so check the NParks notices first, and bring binoculars if you have them.
