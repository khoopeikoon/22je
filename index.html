How it compares

Like a condo master room, each room has its own bathroom. Here the rent also covers utilities, wifi and furniture, a home lift serves every floor, and there is no agent fee. Like a serviced apartment, it is furnished and ready to move in; unlike one, it is let on a standard tenancy agreement from three months, and the kitchen, living room and balcony are shared with the other residents.

It suits working professionals and expats, students with a Student’s Pass (two can share a Deluxe Twin for one rent), and anyone relocating to Singapore.
