How does the booking process work?
  1. Decide which room you’d like and for how long.
  2. Check availability at stay.22je.sg.
  3. Message us on WhatsApp (+65 8016 7691).
  4. We send over a tenancy agreement for you to sign — check the dates carefully (we write dd/mm/yyyy in Singapore).
  5. Send one month’s deposit (SGD or USD preferred; other currencies by discussion).
  6. Your dates turn grey on the occupancy calendar and you receive a confirmation email.
  7. On move-in day you pay the first month’s rent (pro-rated), then on the 1st of each month — or the next business day if the 1st is a weekend or Singapore public holiday.
  8. When you leave, we inspect the room and refund your deposit within 7 days.
What’s included in the rent?

Utilities, high-speed wifi, twice-weekly housekeeping (including laundry), a complimentary amenities kit, and use of the common area and home lift. See Features for the full list.

What information do I need to provide?

It depends on whether you are a resident (Singapore citizen or Permanent Resident) or a non-resident.

Residents:

  • Name of tenant
  • Validity of your Singapore identity card
  • ACRA Unique Entity Number, if you are renting through your company

Non-residents: in addition, the validity of your pass (Employment Pass, S Pass or Work Permit). You can see what landlords are required to check on the CEA website (Step 2) and in CEA’s landlord checklist.

Why is there a 3-month minimum stay?

Singapore’s property-letting rules require a minimum stay of three months for private residential property, so every stay is on a tenancy agreement rather than a nightly booking.

Why can’t I book through Airbnb or other booking sites?

Booking platforms charge a significant fee. When you book directly with us, we pass that saving on to you — and there’s no agent fee either.

And because Singapore’s letting rules require a minimum stay of three months, every stay needs a tenancy agreement signed with us directly.

What tenancy agreement do you use?

We use the government’s standard CEA Tenancy Agreement template for private residential property. Using a standard, government-published contract keeps legal costs out of your rent and means the terms are ones you can check independently.

You are of course welcome to have your own lawyer review it before you sign. You can also find the template on CEA’s agreements and checklists page.

How much is the deposit?

One month’s rent, paid when you sign the tenancy agreement, and refunded within 7 days of move-out after the room is inspected.

How do I know you’re not a scam?

You — or someone you trust — are welcome to view the property before renting. You can also check our reviews (we launched in early 2026, so thank you for your patience while they build up).

For your own safety, read CEA’s guidance on common rental scams.
